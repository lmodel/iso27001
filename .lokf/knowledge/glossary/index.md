# Glossary

* [Information security management system (ISMS)](isms.md) - The organization's managed set of policies, processes, roles and records for information security that ISO/IEC 27001 sets requirements for; the schema's tree root `IsmsManagementSystem` holds one, with its scope and every record it keeps.
* [Annex A control](annex-a-control.md) - One of the 93 reference controls that Annex A of ISO/IEC 27001:2022 lists in four themes; the schema names each by number in the `IsmsAnnexAControl` enum, from `a_5_1` to `a_8_34`, without its title or text.
* [Statement of Applicability](statement-of-applicability.md) - The record that lists each control an organization needs, with why and whether it is in place, and each Annex A control it leaves out, with why; the schema models it as `IsmsStatementOfApplicability`, with `IsmsIncludedControl` and `IsmsExcludedControl` entries.
* [Documented information](documented-information.md) - Information an organization must keep under control, and the medium it is kept on, such as a policy, a procedure or a record; the schema gives such records `IsmsDocumentControlMixin`'s fields for reference, approval, review, classification, retention and change control.
* [Risk treatment](risk-treatment.md) - Choosing and carrying out how to deal with an information security risk: modify it with controls, retain it, avoid it or share it; the schema records the choice on `IsmsRisk` and the work in `IsmsRiskTreatmentPlan`.
* [Nonconformity](nonconformity.md) - A requirement not met, whether of ISO/IEC 27001, of the organization or of an interested party; the schema records one as `IsmsNonconformity`, and the action that removes its cause as `IsmsCorrectiveAction`.
* [Information security event and incident](security-incident.md) - An event is something reported that may bear on information security; an incident is an event assessed as harming it; the schema records an event as `IsmsSecurityEvent` and an incident as its kind `IsmsSecurityIncident`.
