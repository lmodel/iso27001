# Datasets

* [iso27001 LinkML schema](iso27001-linkml-schema.md) - The LinkML schema `src/iso27001/schema/iso27001.yaml`, version `2022/Amd 1:2024`: the records an organization keeps to run an information security management system (ISMS) to ISO/IEC 27001:2022, in 38 classes with the `Isms` prefix, 13 enums including the 93 Annex A controls by number, and its `lokf adapt` copy `.lokf/iso27001.yaml`, rooted on seven classes.
* [ISO Open Data records of ISO/IEC 27001](iso-open-data-records.md) - The pinned extract `src/iso27001/schema/iso-open-data/iso_deliverables_metadata.jsonl`: ISO Open Data's records of ISO/IEC 27001:2022, its Amendment 1:2024, ISO/IEC 27002:2022 and ISO/IEC 27000:2026 as of 2026-10-28, each without its scope, under the ODC Attribution License, which the upstream watch checks weekly.
* [SSSOM mappings to released lmodel schemas](sssom-mappings.md) - The three SSSOM mapping sets under `src/iso27001/mappings/`, 14 class-level rows from the schema's classes to classes of the released lmodel/oscal, lmodel/nist-sp-800-53 and lmodel/spdx schemas, which `just gen-project` merges into the schema, and the sets dropped until their targets are released.
* [Generated artefacts](generated-artefacts.md) - What `just gen-project`, `just gen-project-extended` and `just gen-doc` write from the schema: the Python dataclasses and Pydantic models in `src/iso27001/datamodel/`, the JSON Schema, OWL, SHACL, ShEx, SQL and other files in `project/`, the merged schema in `docs/schema/` and the element pages in `docs/elements/`, none of them edited by hand.
* [Test data and tests](test-data.md) - The schema's tests: 35 valid examples and 47 counter-examples under `tests/data/`, 61 controls derived from Probo's mitigations, and the tests that hold them to the validator, hold the header to ISO Open Data's records, check the upstream watch and the overlay, and keep the adapt copy apart from LOKF and nine released neighbours in `tests/neighbours/`.
