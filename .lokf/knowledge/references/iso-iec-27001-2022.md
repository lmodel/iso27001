# ISO/IEC 27001:2022

ISO/IEC 27001:2022 is the international standard that sets requirements for an information security management system. ISO's record of it, number 82875, gives its title, its third edition, its publication on 2022-10-25, its committee, ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 27, and its stage, 6060, published. ISO's record 88435 is its Amendment 1:2024, Climate action changes, published on 2024-02-23.[^iso-open-data] ISO's page for the edition is <https://www.iso.org/standard/82875.html>, which the schema header names as its `source`.

The standard's text is copyright ISO and IEC, and sold. This repository cites its clauses and Annex A controls by number and quotes none of them.[^rights.md] Its clauses 4 to 10 set the requirements, and Annex A lists 93 reference controls, which ISO/IEC 27002:2022 gives guidance on. The standard refers to ISO/IEC 27000 for its terms.
